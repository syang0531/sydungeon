## Description (본문 — 영문)

**SY Dungeon** builds its dungeons the way vanilla builds villages and trial chambers: from hand-made pieces that the game's own jigsaw generation joins together. Nothing is laid out the same way twice, and every wall is cracked and mossed differently.

It is built around one idea: **you should be able to reach the end of the game by exploring, not by farming and mining.** Beds and torches come out of chests. Obsidian, flint and steel, an enchanting table, eyes of ender — each is the guaranteed reward of a particular dungeon, and each dungeon has exactly one thing that only it gives.

### The overworld: ten large dungeons

#### ⛓️ The Prison — forests and plains
A small stone-brick shelter on the surface with a ladder going down. Two or three shafts deep you land in the hub, and the maze starts: passages, crossroads and stairs on a seven-block grid, several levels deep, dark enough that things spawn in it. Iron-barred cells line the corridors with a chest behind the bars — break them if you want what is inside. One corridor leads somewhere else: a hall three times the size of anything else, five trial spawners, and the **Warden of the Cells** on a zombie horse. Beat him and every player who fought is paid an **enchanted diamond weapon, tool or piece of armour**.

#### 🏜️ The Great Pyramid — deserts
Ninety-one blocks across and forty-six high, a real forty-five degree sandstone pyramid. Through the door on its north face is a nine-by-nine maze of sandstone cells; in the middle of it a well, and a ladder up through the ceiling that puts you in the **middle of the burial chamber floor** with the pharaoh and four spawners around you. There is no door to back out of. Win and you get **fourteen obsidian, flint and steel, and an enchanted diamond piece** — a nether portal and an enchanting table, handed over. Above the chamber's ceiling is one more room with an enchanted golden apple in it.

No pressure plates, no tripwires: mobs do not care whose foot it is, and a trap in a dungeon full of spawners goes off long before you arrive. What is left is danger that needs no trigger — husks buried in sand, and suspicious sand you brush out yourself.

#### 🗼 The Wizard's Tower — taiga
A **round** tower, twenty-nine blocks across and eight floors high. Each floor is nine rooms of seven blocks, and **the stair up stands in a different room on every floor**, so every floor has to be crossed to find it. Junctions have spawners in them; dead ends have chests.

Some floors are a room short. Somewhere on them is a room **with no doors at all** — from the corridor it looks like a doorway that has been bricked up, and the only way in is to notice and dig.

Behind one door on the fourth floor is the **library**: shelves floor to ceiling and an enchanting table with exactly the fifteen that level thirty needs. On the eighth is the **sanctum**, the archmage, and a nether portal frame he never lit. Beating him pays out **four eyes of ender and a totem of undying**.

#### 🧪 The Witch's Swamp — swamps and mangroves
A village on stilts over the water, planks and railings and lanterns, boardwalks running out from a great hut in the middle with a smaller one on the end of each. Some hold a chest; some hold a witch and a boiling cauldron. Through the hatch in the great hut's floor a ladder drops **through the water** into a mud-brick brewery, and three corridors past that is the **Mother of the Swamp** on an island in a flooded hall. Winning gives you a **brewing stand, eight nether wart and four blaze powder** — brewing, opened before you ever see the nether, and the only place in the game this mod gives it.

#### ❄️ The Ice Fortress — snow
A castle forty-nine blocks across on the snow line: a curtain wall round a courtyard, a tower at each corner, a keep in the middle and a gate on the west. Stairs run up from the courtyard onto the wall, so the whole ring can be walked, and every tower has a stray shooting down from it. The keep has three floors — a hall with a hearth and an armoury, a barracks with beds, and the watch on top. The hole in the hall's floor drops **twenty-eight blocks** into deepslate ice cellars: pools of powder snow that swallow you without leather boots, floors of blue ice you cannot stop on, a polar bear behind one door. Three corridors in is the **Frost Lord**, and beating him pays out **an enchanted diamond piece of armour and a Frost Walker II book** — an enchantment no table will ever give you.

#### 🗿 The Temple Swallowed by Vines — jungles
A stepped ziggurat sixty-three blocks across, five terraces of mossy stone with the jungle
growing back over it. A grand stair climbs the south face to a shrine on the summit, which is
given away to anyone willing to walk up the outside. The door is on the north, and through it
is a maze; in the middle of the maze a well, and the well goes two ways - **down into flowing
water channels**, and up into the hall of the **Illusionist Priest**, a mob vanilla itself
never uses. The ladder arrives in the middle of his floor, with the spawners already round
you. Beating him pays **an enchanted diamond weapon and a Fortune III book**.

No pressure plates here either. What is left is infested stone that has to be mined,
suspicious gravel that has to be brushed, and a treasury behind an iron door with a lever - a
mob can tread on a plate, but it cannot throw a switch.

#### 🏕️ The Warlord's Camp — savannas and badlands
A palisade of standing logs forty-nine blocks across, a watchtower at each corner with a
pillager shooting down from it, camp fires and supply chests in the yard, and a longhouse in
the middle with the war table in it. The hole in the longhouse floor drops **twenty-one
blocks** into timbered workings: drifts propped with fence posts, a roof that came down long
ago, suspicious gravel - and a **ravager in an iron cage**, which is a choice rather than a
trap, because nothing but a player breaks iron bars on purpose. Three corridors in is the
**Warlord**, and he pays out **a crossbow with multishot or piercing, ominous bottles,
emeralds and two sentry armour trim templates**. The bottles are how a vanilla trial chamber
is opened ominously, and this is the only place the mod gives them.

#### ⚰️ The Cursed Graveyard — dark forests and pale gardens
A ruined chapel with the plots spreading out from it - headstones, a grave someone dug and
never filled, little stone tombs, pale dead trees with a **creaking heart** in the trunk - and
a single panel of iron railing wherever a row runs out. The hole in the chapel floor drops
**twenty-eight blocks** into an ossuary cut out of the rock: sealed coffins, soul soil that
slows you to a walk, bone shelves. Three corridors in is the **Horseman Captain**, and beating
him gives **a Mending book and a saddle** - with a stable of **tame skeleton horses** standing
in his hall, still there when the fight is over. Mending is a treasure enchantment: no
enchanting table will ever give you one, and in this mod nowhere else will either.

#### ⛏️ The Dwarven Hall — peaks and windswept hills
A deepslate gatehouse with a great arch, and that door is the only part of it in daylight.
The shaft in its floor drops **forty-two blocks** before the hall begins, because a mountain
is the steepest ground in the game and a dungeon that sprawls across one ends up standing on
stilts. Down there: forges with anvils and troughs of lava set flush with the floor, drifts
laid with mine cart rail and timbered with spruce, and a **chasm with lava at the bottom**
whose span narrows to a single block in the middle. The ore is in solid rock and a pick is the
only way into it - which is the one thing a wandering mob cannot do on your behalf. Three
corridors in is the **Forge King** with a diamond axe, his smiths are pick-carrying zombies
and breezes, and beating him pays out an **efficiency IV, unbreaking III diamond pickaxe or
axe, two armour trim templates and an anvil**.

#### 🌊 The Sunken Lighthouse — beaches and stony shores
A broken tower on the sand, climbable to the lamp room at the top where two sea lanterns sit
in a ring of weathered copper. The hole in its floor drops twenty-one blocks to a dry sump,
and the hole in **that** floor has the sea two blocks below the lip. You jump in and swim
down into a prismarine fortress that is full of water: guardians, drowned with tridents, kelp
growing from the floor, a soul sand bubble column that carries you up, a sponge room with dry
sponges in the chest. The boss hall is drained to head height - six courses of sea and air
above them - so the **Deep Sentinel** can be fought at the surface. He gives up a **trident
with loyalty or riptide, and the heart of the sea**. Every chest's first roll can be water
breathing, because down there that is not a consumable, it is the ticket in.

### The Nether: three

#### 🔥 The Blaze Fortress — nether wastes, basalt deltas
Nether brick and blackstone over a
moat of lava, with bridges across it and blazes on the spires. Wither skeletons hold the
inside. Three corridors in is the **Lord of Flame**, and he pays out **eight blaze rods and a
wither skeleton skull** — eight rods is sixteen powder, so two trips fills the twelve eyes of
ender, and the skull is there if you ever want the other boss vanilla has.

#### 🪙 The Piglin Vault — crimson and warped forests
Blackstone and gold underground, laid
out in cages and strongrooms. Mine the gold and the piglins turn on you. Beating the
**Vaultkeeper** gives **four netherite scrap and a netherite upgrade smithing template**, and
this is the **only source of netherite in the mod**: one piece of gear upgraded per vault
cleared.

#### 💀 The Soul Sanctum — soul sand valley
Soul soil, bone blocks and soul lanterns burning
blue. The sand holds your feet and the fire burns what stands in it. The **Voice of the
Sanctum** gives up a **Soul Speed III book and an enchanted golden apple**. There are no
ghasts in the boss hall on purpose — a fireball takes the hall apart while you are still in it.

### The End: two

#### 🌌 The Void Spire — end highlands, after the dragon
Purpur and end stone brick standing
over the void, and **the only dungeon in the mod that goes up instead of down**. Shulkers levitate
you off the walkway; one gap can only be crossed with a pearl. At the top, the **Heart of the
Spire** pays out **an elytra and two shulker shells** — the end of travel, and the start of
shulker boxes.

#### 🟣 The Chorus Labyrinth — end midlands
A maze of end stone among the chorus stalks with
**no roof on it**: the void is simply there beside you, and eating chorus fruit puts you
somewhere else. A pack of endermen guards **sixteen ender pearls**, guaranteed. The boss hall
alone is roofed — an enderman that teleports out of the fight is one the trial spawner will
wait for forever.

### The small ones: twelve

Ten minutes each. **No boss**, and what they hand over is what you actually need on the first
night — a bed, torches, food, and enough iron to keep moving. If the big dungeons are a day
out, these are the hole you fall into on the way.

#### ⛩️ The Mountain Shrine — cherry groves
**No boss, and nothing in it is hostile** - no trial spawner, no mob spawner, not one. Four
paved paths run out through the blossom under torii gates, past benches, gardens and a hot
spring, and where they meet is a walled court with a plinth seven blocks high and a shrine on
top of it. The chest on the altar holds **six ender pearls and eight bottles of experience**,
guaranteed. That is the only place in this mod where a chest hands over progression, and it
is allowed because the shrine asks nothing of you in return. Inside the plinth is a cellar
**with no way in at all** - the only clue is that the plinth is wider than the shrine standing
on it.

#### 🍄 The Mycelium Hollow — mushroom fields
The small one. A hole in the mycelium ringed by four huge mushrooms, a short ladder, and a
burrow lit by glow lichen. **Nothing down there will hurt you.** A mooshroom pen and a camp
with two beds are both guaranteed to generate, and the chests hold bowls, stew and a bed. Ten
minutes, no boss, no signature reward - what it gives you is somewhere to stop.


The other ten come out of **one machine wearing different skins**. Whether it is a well or a
cairn, the inside is the same short maze; what changes is what it is built of and what lives
in it.

| Dungeon | Where | What lives in it |
|---|---|---|
| The Hidden Well | plains, forests, birch, meadow | zombies |
| The Tomb Under the Sand | desert | husks |
| The Hunter's Cabin | the three taigas | skeletons |
| The Cairn in the Snow | the six snowy biomes | strays |
| The Dry Well | savannas and badlands | husks |
| The Store Under the Vines | the three jungles | cave spiders |
| The Forester's Cellar | dark forest, pale garden | zombie villagers |
| The Flooded Store | swamps and mangroves | drowned |
| The Mossy Burrow | lush caves | cave spiders |
| The Dripstone Burrow | dripstone caves | skeletons |

The last two have **nothing at all on the surface** — a single room set in the rock, found by
digging into it.

Between them the mod covers forty-two of the forty-four overworld land biomes, all five of
the Nether's, and both large End islands.

### 📦 What the chests hold
Every chest's first roll is survival kit — a bed, torches, food, coal — because a dungeon this dark eats torches and there is no farm to sleep beside. The rest is iron, gold, books, the occasional diamond. **No diamond gear in any chest.** That is what the bosses are for.

### 🛠️ Shapes you can change without code
Every piece is a structure-block save. Build a better crossroads in creative, save it under the same name, and the mod's tooling holds it against the one it replaces — same size, same jigsaws, doorways still open — before it goes in. The rules that join the pieces, the loot, the spawner waves and the weathering are all data-pack JSON. The tower you are walking through was drawn by hand in a creative world, floor by floor.

### Requirements
- **Minecraft 26.2**
- **NeoForge 26.2.0.88**
- **Java 25**
- No other mods required. Works on dedicated servers.

### Before you uninstall
Dungeons are ordinary vanilla blocks and stay standing. The only thing this mod registers is a structure type, so a world opened without it keeps every dungeon that already exists and simply stops generating new ones.

## Description (본문 — 한글, 추가 언어용)

**SY Dungeon**은 바닐라가 마을과 트라이얼 챔버를 짓는 방식 그대로 던전을 짓습니다. 손으로 만든 조각을 게임의 직소 생성이 이어 붙입니다. 두 번 같은 배치가 나오지 않고, 벽은 매번 다르게 금 가고 이끼 낍니다.

목적은 하나입니다. **파밍과 채굴 없이, 탐험만으로 게임의 끝에 닿을 수 있어야 한다.** 침대와 횃불은 상자에서 나옵니다. 흑요석, 화염과 강철, 마법 부여대, 엔더의 눈 — 전부 특정 던전의 확정 보상이고, 던전마다 **거기서만 나오는 것**이 하나씩 있습니다.

### 오버월드 — 대규모 열

**⛓️ 지하감옥 — 숲과 평원** 지표의 작은 돌벽돌 정자에서 사다리가 내려갑니다. 수직통로를 두세 번 갈아타면 허브고, 거기서 미로가 시작됩니다. 7칸 격자의 통로·교차로·계단이 여러 층으로 이어지고, 어두워서 몹이 저절로 생깁니다. 통로 옆 철창 뒤 감방에는 상자가 있고, 갖고 싶으면 철창을 깨야 합니다. 복도 하나는 다른 곳으로 이어집니다 — 미로의 어떤 방보다 세 배 큰 홀, 트라이얼 스포너 다섯, 좀비말을 탄 **간수장**. 다 잡으면 싸운 플레이어 전원에게 **마법 부여 다이아 무기·도구·방어구**가 나옵니다.

**🏜️ 대피라미드 — 사막** 밑변 91칸, 높이 46칸의 진짜 45° 사암 피라미드. 북면의 문으로 들어가면 9×9칸 미로가 있고, 그 한가운데 우물의 사다리가 천장을 뚫고 **묘실 바닥 한복판**으로 올려보냅니다. 파라오와 스포너 넷이 사방에 있고, 물러날 문이 없습니다. 이기면 **흑요석 14 + 화염과 강철 + 마법 부여 다이아 장비** — 네더 포탈과 마법 부여대를 통째로 받습니다. 묘실 천장 위에는 마법 황금사과가 든 석관실이 하나 더 있습니다.

압력판도 철사도 없습니다. 몹은 누구 발인지 가리지 않아서, 스포너가 가득한 던전의 함정은 플레이어가 오기 훨씬 전에 터집니다. 남은 것은 트리거가 필요 없는 위험뿐입니다 — 모래에 묻힌 허스크, 직접 솔질해 파내는 수상한 모래.

**🗼 마법사의 탑 — 타이가** 밑변 29칸의 **둥근** 탑, 여덟 층. 한 층은 7칸짜리 방 아홉 개고, **올라가는 계단이 층마다 다른 방에 있어서** 매 층 걸어서 찾아야 합니다. 거리 한가운데에는 스포너가, 막다른 방에는 상자가 있습니다.

어떤 층은 방이 하나 모자랍니다. 거기 어딘가에 **문이 하나도 없는 방**이 있습니다. 복도에서는 돌로 막힌 문으로 보이고, 눈치채고 파야만 들어갑니다.

4층 문 너머는 **도서관**입니다. 바닥부터 천장까지 책장이고, 마법 부여대 둘레에 30레벨에 필요한 열다섯 개가 정확히 놓여 있습니다. 8층은 **성소**, 대마법사, 그리고 그가 끝내 불붙이지 않은 네더 포탈 틀. 그를 이기면 **엔더의 눈 넷과 불사의 토템**이 나옵니다.

**🧪 마녀의 늪 — 늪과 맹그로브 늪** 물 위에 말뚝을 박고 지은 마을입니다. 판자와 난간과 등불, 가운데 큰 오두막에서 널다리가 사방으로 뻗고 끝마다 작은 오두막이 달립니다. 상자가 든 곳도, 가마솥을 끓이는 마녀가 있는 곳도 있습니다. 큰 오두막 바닥의 해치로 사다리가 **물을 뚫고** 내려가면 진흙 벽돌 양조장이고, 통로 셋 뒤에 물이 찬 홀의 섬 위로 **늪의 어머니**가 서 있습니다. 이기면 **양조기와 네더 사마귀 여덟, 블레이즈 가루 넷** — 네더를 보기도 전에 양조가 열립니다. 이 모드에서 그것을 주는 곳은 여기뿐입니다.

**❄️ 얼음 성채 — 눈 바이옴** 눈 위에 선 밑변 49칸의 성입니다. 성벽이 안뜰을 두르고 귀퉁이마다 탑이, 가운데 아성이, 서쪽에 성문이 있습니다. 안뜰에서 계단으로 순찰로에 올라 성을 한 바퀴 돌 수 있고, 탑마다 스트레이가 쏘아 옵니다. 아성은 세 층 — 화덕과 무기고가 있는 홀, 침대가 놓인 병사 방, 그리고 망루. 홀 바닥의 구멍으로 **스물여덟 칸** 내려가면 심층암 얼음 창고입니다. 가죽 부츠 없이는 빠지는 가루눈 웅덩이, 멈출 수 없는 푸른 얼음 바닥, 문 하나 뒤의 북극곰. 통로 셋 뒤에 **서리 군주**가 있고, 이기면 **마법 부여 다이아 방어구 한 조각과 서리 걷기 II 책**이 나옵니다 — 마법 부여대는 절대 주지 않는 마법입니다.

**⚰️ 저주받은 묘지 — 어두운숲과 창백한 정원** 무너진 예배당에서 묘역이 사방으로 뻗습니다 — 묘비, 누군가 파고 끝내 덮지 않은 무덤, 작은 석실, 줄기 속에 **삐걱 심장**이 든 창백한 죽은 나무. 줄이 끊기는 자리에는 철 울타리 한 장이 섭니다. 예배당 바닥의 구멍으로 **28칸** 내려가면 바위를 파낸 납골당입니다 — 봉인된 석관, 걸음을 붙잡는 영혼 흙, 뼈 선반. 통로 셋 뒤의 **기병대장**을 이기면 **수선 책과 안장**이 나오고, 그의 홀에는 **길들여진 스켈레톤말**이 싸움이 끝난 뒤에도 그대로 서 있습니다. 수선은 보물 마법입니다 — 마법 부여대는 절대 주지 않고, 이 모드에서도 여기밖에 없습니다.

**🌊 침몰한 등대 — 해변과 자갈 해안** 모래 위에 부서진 탑이 서 있고, 꼭대기 등실까지 올라가면 풍화된 구리 테 안에 바다 랜턴 둘이 있습니다. 바닥 구멍으로 21칸 내려가면 마른 집수실이고, **그 바닥의 구멍** 아래 두 칸이 수면입니다. 뛰어들어 헤엄쳐 내려가면 물로 가득한 프리즈머린 요새입니다 — 가디언, 삼지창을 든 드라운드, 바닥에서 올라오는 다시마, 사람을 밀어 올리는 소울 모래 기포 기둥, 마른 해면이 든 상자. 보스방은 물이 여섯 켜에 그 위가 공기라 **수면 위에서** 싸울 수 있습니다. **심해의 파수꾼**을 이기면 **충성 또는 급류 삼지창과 해양의 심장**이 나옵니다. 상자의 첫 굴림에 물 호흡 물약이 나올 수 있습니다 — 여기서 그것은 소모품이 아니라 통행권입니다.

**💀 영혼 성소 — 영혼 모래 골짜기** 영혼 흙과 뼈 블록, 푸르게 타는 영혼 랜턴. 모래는 발을 붙잡고 불은 서 있는 것을 태웁니다. **성소의 목소리**를 이기면 **영혼 속도 III 책과 마법 황금사과**가 나옵니다. 보스 홀에 가스트가 없는 것은 일부러입니다 — 화염구가 싸우고 있는 홀을 부숩니다.

**🟣 코러스 미궁 — 엔드 중지** 코러스 줄기 사이에 엔드 돌로 짠 미로고, **지붕이 없습니다.** 공허가 옆에 그대로 있고, 코러스 과일을 먹으면 어딘가 다른 곳에 떨어집니다. 엔더맨 무리를 이기면 **엔더 진주 열여섯**이 확정입니다. 보스 홀만 지붕이 있는데, 순간이동으로 빠져나간 엔더맨은 트라이얼 스포너가 영원히 기다리기 때문입니다.

### 중소규모 — 열둘

한 판 십 분입니다. **보스가 없고**, 주는 것은 첫날 밤에 실제로 필요한 것 — 침대, 횃불, 음식, 그리고 움직일 만큼의 철입니다. 큰 던전이 찾아가는 하루라면 이것은 가는 길에 빠지는 구멍입니다.

**⛩️ 산신의 사당 — 벚꽃숲** **보스가 없고, 적대적인 것이 하나도 없습니다.** 트라이얼 스포너도 몬스터 스포너도 없습니다. 토리이가 선 돌길 넷이 꽃그늘로 뻗고, 쉼터와 정원과 온천을 지나면 담을 두른 마당이 나옵니다. 가운데 일곱 칸 단상 위 사당의 제단 상자에는 **엔더 진주 여섯과 경험치 병 여덟**이 확정으로 들어 있습니다. 이 모드에서 상자가 진행을 주는 곳은 여기 하나뿐이고, 사당이 아무것도 요구하지 않기 때문에 허용한 예외입니다. 그리고 단상 속에는 **문이 하나도 없는 지하실**이 있습니다 — 단상이 그 위의 사당보다 넓다는 것이 유일한 단서입니다.
